About Rallybit

A better rhythm for community management.

Rallybit was built to make activity checks useful, fair, and much easier for staff teams to run.

Why it exists

Community staff often spend too much time repeatedly posting checks, counting responses, chasing inactive members, and rebuilding the same reports. Rallybit brings those tasks into one focused system.

What makes it different

There are no feature tiers. Automation, logs, custom checks, leaderboards, profiles, and analytics are included for every server. The goal is simple: give communities a reliable toolkit without locking everyday moderation tools behind artificial access levels.

Designed around clarity

Every feature is built to answer a practical question: who participated, when did they respond, what happened during the check, and what should staff do next?

Still evolving

Rallybit is actively developed. Feedback, bug reports, and feature suggestions help shape the roadmap and keep the product centred on the communities using it.
