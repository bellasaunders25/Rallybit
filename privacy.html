Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 July 2026

Information Rallybit processes

Rallybit may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, usernames, server names, check settings, participation events, winner totals, command usage, and technical diagnostic information required to operate the service.

Why this information is used

The data is used to run activity checks, generate leaderboards and profiles, provide audit logs, save server settings, prevent abuse, troubleshoot faults, and improve reliability.

Discord authentication

The dashboard uses Discord OAuth to identify you and determine which servers you are allowed to manage. Rallybit does not receive your Discord password.

Retention

Operational records are kept only for as long as they are needed to provide the requested features, maintain security, or resolve technical issues. Administrators should provide a deletion route for server data when requested.

Sharing

Rallybit does not sell personal information. Data may be handled by essential infrastructure providers where needed to host and operate the service, or disclosed where legally required.

Your choices

You may stop further collection by removing Rallybit from a server. Requests to access or delete stored data should be made through the official support server.

Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect stored information. No internet service can guarantee complete security, so credentials and deployment secrets must be kept private and rotated if exposed.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Rallybit changes. Material changes should be announced through the website or support community.
