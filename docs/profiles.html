Member recognition

Turn participation into a visible history.

Rallybit profiles combine wins, check-ins, ranks, badges, and optional member details in one shareable page.

What a profile shows

Wins

The number of activity checks where the member was selected as a winner.

Check-ins

The member’s total recorded participation across supported checks.

Rank

A simple progression title based on the member’s total wins.

Badges

Milestone and community achievements assigned through Rallybit.

Find a profile

Use /profile, open /dashboard/profile.php, or visit the clean URL /id/USERNAME.

Edit your details

Sign in to the dashboard, open Account, then add a short bio or website. Discord identity and participation statistics remain controlled by Rallybit.
