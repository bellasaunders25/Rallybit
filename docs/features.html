Product guide · Build 7.2.2

One focused toolkit for active, well-run communities.

Rallybit combines recurring engagement, lightweight community games, transparent moderation, useful reports, and member recognition without splitting features into paid tiers.

Manual activity checks

Launch a timed check with a custom message, reaction or button, participant target, and staff permissions.

Recurring check automation

Schedule activity checks by channel and interval, then pause or resume them without deleting the setup.

Scheduled community quizzes

Run random category-based quizzes manually or automatically, notify an optional quiz role, keep answers hidden until close, and rank members by points and accuracy.

Interactive moderation and reports

Manage warnings, reports, timeouts, kicks, bans, message clearing, nicknames, and roles with action-specific role permissions.

Persistent support tickets

Create, claim, prioritise, transcribe, close, and reopen support tickets with a clear six-stage workflow.

Staff and member reviews

Collect structured one-to-five-star reviews and route each review type to its own configured channel.

AFK status

Let members mark themselves away, show their reason when mentioned, and clear the status automatically when they return.

Anonymous community pulses

Notify an optional Chat Revive role, collect aggregate mood responses, and avoid publishing or retaining individual response identities in pulse history.

Conversation starters

Post casual, gaming, creative, and debate prompts when chat needs a gentle nudge.

Audit logs

Review check status, host, participant totals, winners, timestamps, warnings, and moderation actions.

Leaderboards

Rank members by activity check-ins, wins, quiz points, accuracy, and quiz streaks.

Member profiles

Give each member a public participation profile with stats, rank, bio, and achievements.

Server analytics

Track activity trends and see how member participation changes over time.

Community quiz behaviour

StageBehaviour
OpenMembers choose one answer. Their choice is acknowledged privately and remains hidden from the channel.
CloseRallybit reveals the correct answer, explanation, fastest correct responses, and round totals.
LeagueCorrect answers earn base points plus a speed bonus. Accuracy and best streak are retained per server.

Moderation safety controls

Rallybit never applies moderation automatically. Every action requires an explicit moderator interaction, the relevant Discord permission, a valid role hierarchy, and sufficient bot permissions. Actions performed through Rallybit are recorded in moderation history.

Community pulse privacy

Responses are held only for the active pulse so members can change their selection. After close, Rallybit stores aggregate totals and does not store the list of members behind each response.

Configurable security suite

Rallybit includes a top-level scam honeypot, account-age gate, unauthorised bot-add protection, anti-nuke thresholds, webhook and permission protection, anti-spam, optional automod, quarantine, lockdown, emergency invite purging, persistent security history, and granular per-server settings.
