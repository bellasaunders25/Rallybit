Results

How Rallybit selects and records winners.

Winners are chosen from eligible participants at the end of a check and added to the activity history.

Selection flow

Members participate

Each valid member can join through the selected reaction or button.

The check closes

Rallybit stops accepting entries when the timer ends or staff stop it manually.

Eligible winners are selected

The bot chooses up to the configured winner count without duplicating a member.

Results are recorded

Winners, participation totals, status, and timing are added to logs and statistics.

Fairness notes

Staff should announce any extra eligibility rules before a check begins. Rallybit handles random selection from valid participants but cannot judge rules that exist only outside the bot.

No-winner checks

If nobody participates, Rallybit closes the check without selecting a winner and still records the outcome for staff review.
